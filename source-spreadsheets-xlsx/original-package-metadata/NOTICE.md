# Source, attribution, and rights notice

## Australian Bureau of Statistics material

This database is an adapted compilation of aggregate statistics published by the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS):

- [Criminal Courts, Australia](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/crime-and-justice/criminal-courts-australia/2024-25), accepted 2020–21 through 2024–25 and source-only 2009–10, 2010–11, 2012–13, 2014–15 and 2015–16 release vintages
- [Federal Defendants, Australia](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/crime-and-justice/federal-defendants-australia/2024-25), source-only 2013–14 through 2018–19 and accepted 2020–21 through 2024–25 release vintages
- [Prisoners in Australia](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/crime-and-justice/prisoners-australia/2025), source-only 2006 through 2018 and accepted 2020 through 2025 release vintages
- [Recorded Crime – Offenders](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/crime-and-justice/recorded-crime-offenders/2024-25), source-only 2010–11 through 2017–18 and accepted 2020–21 through 2024–25 release vintages
- [Employee Earnings and Hours, Australia, May 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/labour/earnings-and-working-conditions/employee-earnings-and-hours-australia/may-2021), released 19 January 2022
- [Employee Earnings and Hours, Australia, May 2023](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/labour/earnings-and-working-conditions/employee-earnings-and-hours-australia/may-2023), released 24 January 2024 with data cubes 3 and 8 updated 21 February 2024
- [Employee Earnings and Hours, Australia, May 2025](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/labour/earnings-and-working-conditions/employee-earnings-and-hours-australia/may-2025), released 23 January 2026

Suggested attribution:

> Source: Australian Bureau of Statistics, *Criminal Courts, Australia*, *Federal Defendants, Australia*, *Prisoners in Australia*, *Recorded Crime – Offenders*, and *Employee Earnings and Hours, Australia* for May 2021, May 2023, and May 2025. Original acquisition dates are retained in source-custody records. Based on ABS data and transformed/adapted into a consolidated SQLite compilation by Ian Moran.

Source release vintage and observation period remain distinct. Classification contexts change across releases, and Employee Earnings and Hours dimensions include employment, earnings, hours, pay-setting, occupation, industry, sector, geography, and employer characteristics. Consult the ABS source notes before comparing or combining unlike populations, measures, units, classifications, or periods.

The October 2026 historical extensions use retained original acquisitions, freshly verified offline; original custody records retain the exact acquisition dates. Historical ASOC/ANZSOC classifications, federal jurisdiction exclusions, defendant counting entities and methodology, perturbation/confidentialisation, and revised historical figures are edition-specific. Different vintages may restate the same observation period and must not be treated as independent people or events. Literal month, quarter, revision and change-interval labels remain literal; no exact date or harmonized semantic unit is invented where the source does not supply one. The Prisoners 2012 metadata uses the retained 2 April 2013 reissue date.

Prisoners 2018 uses corrected v2 source context: eight Table_34 statewide totals no longer inherit a Females heading. The original values and counts are unchanged. Five complete Criminal Courts source packages were independently qualified for this release; they are a gapped series, not continuous annual coverage. Courts 2018–19 remains deferred for unresolved Table 14 mean/median ancestry/note scope. Offenders 2008–09 and Federal Defendants 2011–12 are excluded for retained-original checksum mismatches; other partial/blocked Courts and RoGS packages are not included. This extension covers the selected spreadsheet table packages, not additional HTML/PDF-only material or accepted semantic assets.

Historical Courts source pages: [2009–10](https://www.abs.gov.au/AUSSTATS/abs%40.nsf/DetailsPage/4513.02009-10?OpenDocument), [2010–11](https://www.abs.gov.au/AUSSTATS/abs%40.nsf/DetailsPage/4513.02010-11?OpenDocument), [2012–13](https://www.abs.gov.au/AUSSTATS/abs%40.nsf/DetailsPage/4513.02012-13?OpenDocument), [2014–15](https://www.abs.gov.au/AUSSTATS/abs%40.nsf/DetailsPage/4513.02014-15?OpenDocument), [2015–16](https://www.abs.gov.au/AUSSTATS/abs%40.nsf/DetailsPage/4513.02015-16?OpenDocument). Native spreadsheet inventories, qualified statuses, source units and source notes are retained without harmonizing defendant/offence counting bases.

The 2021 Employee Earnings and Hours survey was postponed from May 2020 because of the labour-market disruption from the first wave of the COVID-19 pandemic. The May 2021 individual downloads currently served by ABS and their official combined archive have equal worksheet inventories, formulas, cached values, and semantic cells, although 11 XLSX container files are not byte-identical. The database uses the current individual downloads and retains the combined archive with that verified semantic-equivalence relationship.

The Commonwealth of Australia owns copyright in ABS-produced material. ABS states that website material is available under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), except for specified exclusions. See the [ABS website privacy, copyright and disclaimer](https://www.abs.gov.au/website-privacy-copyright-and-disclaimer) and [How to cite ABS sources](https://www.abs.gov.au/how-cite-abs-sources).

ABS exclusions include the Commonwealth Coat of Arms, ABS logo, trademarks, unit-record data (microdata), third-party content, and specified branding/artwork. Those materials are not intentionally included. The database contains transformed aggregate published statistics, raw labels, source-cell provenance, and publication metadata—not unit records or direct personal identifiers.

## Transformations and authority

Transformations include semantic row selection, explicit dimensional modelling, typed values, status-marker preservation, source fingerprinting, and provenance linkage. Raw labels, workbook digests, worksheet names, source cells, release vintages, and observation periods are retained for auditability.

The source-cell rows have checked publication-wide extraction evidence but are not represented as accepted worksheet-assets. The database is a replaceable query projection and is not acceptance authority.
